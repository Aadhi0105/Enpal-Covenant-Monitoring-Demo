FIRST AMENDMENT TO TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT AND WAIVER

This FIRST AMENDMENT TO TERM LOAN CREDIT AGREEMENT AND WAIVER (this "Amendment") is made and entered into as of April 11, 2025, among HELIORA GROUP HOLDCO GmbH, a limited liability company organized under the laws of Germany (the "Borrower"), HELIORA ENERGY GmbH, as guarantor (the "Guarantor"), ARDENNE CAPITAL BANK AG, as administrative agent (the "Administrative Agent"), and the Required Lenders party hereto.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Borrower, the Guarantor, the Administrative Agent, and the Lenders are parties to that certain Term Loan Credit Agreement, dated as of June 3, 2023 (as in effect immediately prior to the date hereof, the "Existing Credit Agreement"; as amended by this Amendment, the "Credit Agreement");

WHEREAS, the Borrower previously exercised its cure right under Section 6.02 of the Existing Credit Agreement with respect to the Test Period ended December 31, 2024;

WHEREAS, the Borrower has advised the Administrative Agent that, absent the relief provided by this Amendment, it anticipates reporting a Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio in excess of 3.50:1.00 for the Test Period ending March 31, 2025 (the "Specified Default"), and that it does not intend to exercise a further cure right under Section 6.02 with respect to such Test Period;

WHEREAS, the Borrower has requested that the Required Lenders (a) waive the Specified Default and (b) amend Section 6.01 of the Existing Credit Agreement on the terms set forth herein; and

WHEREAS, the Administrative Agent and the Required Lenders are willing to grant such waiver and to so amend the Existing Credit Agreement, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Amendment;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

Section 1. Defined Terms. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings assigned to them in the Existing Credit Agreement.

Section 2. Waiver. Subject to the satisfaction of the conditions set forth in Section 4 of this Amendment, the Required Lenders hereby waive the Specified Default. This waiver is limited to the Specified Default as and to the extent described above, applies solely to the Test Period ending March 31, 2025, and shall not be construed as a waiver of any other Default or Event of Default, whether now existing or hereafter arising, or of the Borrower's obligation to comply with Section 6.01 as amended hereby for any subsequent Test Period.

Section 3. Amendment to Section 6.01. Section 6.01 of the Existing Credit Agreement is hereby amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

"Section 6.01. Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio, as of the last day of any Test Period, to exceed the ratio set forth below opposite the period in which such Test Period ends:

Test Periods ending on or after September 30, 2023 and on or before December 31, 2024: 3.50:1.00

Test Periods ending on or after March 31, 2025: 4.00:1.00"

For the avoidance of doubt: (a) the Consolidated Net Leverage Ratio covenant as set forth in Section 6.01 of the Existing Credit Agreement, without giving effect to this Amendment, remains the governing covenant, and the applicable maximum ratio remains 3.50:1.00, with respect to each Test Period ended on or before December 31, 2024; and (b) the amended covenant set forth in this Section 3, with a maximum ratio of 4.00:1.00, governs each Test Period ending on or after March 31, 2025, including the Test Period ending March 31, 2025 itself, notwithstanding that the Specified Default relating to that Test Period arose, and is waived, under the ratio in effect prior to this Amendment.

Section 4. Conditions to Effectiveness. This Amendment shall become effective as of the date first written above (the "Amendment Effective Date") upon satisfaction of the following conditions: (a) receipt by the Administrative Agent of counterparts of this Amendment duly executed by the Borrower, the Guarantor, the Administrative Agent, and the Required Lenders; (b) receipt by the Administrative Agent of a certificate of a Responsible Officer of the Borrower certifying that, after giving effect to this Amendment, no Default or Event of Default other than the Specified Default has occurred and is continuing; and (c) payment by the Borrower of all fees and expenses owing to the Administrative Agent in connection with this Amendment.

Section 5. Representations and Warranties. The Borrower and the Guarantor each represent and warrant to the Administrative Agent and the Lenders, as of the Amendment Effective Date, that (a) the execution, delivery, and performance of this Amendment are within its corporate powers and have been duly authorized, (b) this Amendment constitutes its legal, valid, and binding obligation, and (c) other than the Specified Default, no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing.

Section 6. No Other Waiver or Amendment. Except as expressly set forth in this Amendment, the Existing Credit Agreement and the other Loan Documents remain unmodified and in full force and effect, and nothing herein shall be construed as a waiver of any right or remedy of the Administrative Agent or the Lenders with respect to any Default or Event of Default other than the Specified Default, whether now existing or hereafter arising. This Amendment shall constitute a Loan Document.

Section 7. Governing Law. This Amendment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Germany, without regard to conflicts of laws principles.

Section 8. Counterparts. This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be an original, and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Amendment to be executed as of the date first written above.
